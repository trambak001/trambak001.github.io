About

From Gujarat — thinking in numbers, writing in stories.

I started in finance because I wanted to understand how systems work. I started writing because I wanted to explain those systems to people who don't live inside spreadsheets. That combination — rigorous analysis + clear narrative — is the thread that runs through everything I do.

Today I work as a freelance analyst and financial writer. I've built financial models, written market research reports, published a book, and contributed articles on money, markets, and economic life in India. I'm based in Gujarat and work with clients worldwide.

I believe the best financial insight is the one that changes how people act in real life, not just what looks good in a spreadsheet.
